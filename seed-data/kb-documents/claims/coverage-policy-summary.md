# Coverage Policy Summary (Demo Plans)

This is a simplified summary of coverage rules across the three demo plans
used in this system. It is written for the Claims specialist agent to
ground answers about what is or isn't typically covered — it is not a legal
plan document.

## Gold PPO (PLAN-PPO-GOLD-01)
- No referral required to see in-network specialists.
- Out-of-network care is covered at a reduced coinsurance rate; still
  requires the claim to be filed correctly.
- Preventive/annual wellness visits are covered at 100% with no member
  cost-share when billed as preventive.

## Silver HMO (PLAN-HMO-SILVER-02)
- Referral from the member's PCP is required before seeing most
  specialists; claims for specialist visits without an on-file referral are
  a common source of CO-197 denials under this plan.
- Out-of-network care (except emergencies) is generally not covered at all
  under this plan type.

## Bronze PPO (PLAN-PPO-BRONZE-03)
- Higher member cost-share (deductible/coinsurance) than Gold, but broader
  network flexibility than the Silver HMO.
- Certain elective and cosmetic procedures are excluded from coverage
  entirely regardless of medical opinion — these typically deny as CO-96.

## General notes
- Imaging studies (MRI, CT) above a certain cost threshold typically require
  prior authorization on all three plans.
- Behavioral health visits are covered on all three plans at parity with
  medical/surgical benefits.
