# Appointment Scheduling — FAQ

## How far in advance can a member book an appointment?
Routine appointments can generally be booked up to 90 days in advance.
Urgent/sick visits are typically booked within 24-72 hours depending on
provider availability.

## What's the difference between telehealth and in-person visits in this
system?
Telehealth visits are available for follow-ups, medication management, and
behavioral health check-ins. New-patient visits, procedures, and most
diagnostic services require an in-person visit.

## Can a member switch a scheduled in-person visit to telehealth?
In most cases yes, if the provider offers telehealth for that visit type
and it's changed at least 24 hours before the appointment. This counts as a
reschedule for policy purposes (see cancellation-rescheduling-policy.md).

## What if a member has no upcoming appointments and wants to book one?
This demo's scheduling tool surface supports looking up and rescheduling
existing appointments, but not booking a brand-new appointment from
scratch — the agent should let the member know a new appointment can be
booked by calling the office directly, and offer to check the member's
provider's contact info if useful.
