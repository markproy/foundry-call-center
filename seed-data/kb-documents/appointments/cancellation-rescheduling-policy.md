# Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

## Standard rescheduling window
Appointments can be rescheduled online or by phone up to 24 hours before
the scheduled time without any restriction, subject to the new slot's
availability.

## Same-day reschedule requests
Same-day reschedule or cancellation requests are **not eligible** for
self-service rescheduling through the agent or portal. A member requesting
a same-day change must be directed to call the provider's office directly,
since same-day slot changes often require manual coordination with the
provider's in-office schedule. When the agent sees an appointment record
with `reschedule_eligible: false` and a same-day scheduled time, this is
the policy reason — the agent should explain this plainly rather than
attempting the reschedule tool call.

## No-show policy
Two or more no-shows within a 12-month period may result in a requirement
to schedule only through the office directly (not via self-service
channels) for a cooling-off period. This is informational context only —
the demo's simulated data does not track no-show history.

## Cancelling without rescheduling
A member can cancel without picking a new time; the agent should confirm
whether they'd like to be offered a new time before completing a pure
cancellation, since most members calling to "cancel" actually want to move
to a different time.
