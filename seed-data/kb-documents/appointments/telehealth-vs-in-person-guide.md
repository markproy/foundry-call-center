# Telehealth vs. In-Person Visit Guide

## When telehealth is appropriate
- Medication management follow-ups
- Behavioral health check-ins
- Routine follow-up visits where no physical exam component is required
- Lab result review appointments

## When in-person is required
- New patient visits (a baseline physical exam is required)
- Any visit involving a procedure, injection, or imaging
- Visits where the provider has flagged an in-person exam is needed based on
  the member's last visit notes

## Cost differences
Telehealth visits generally have the same member cost-share as an
equivalent in-person visit under all three demo plans — there is no
telehealth discount or surcharge modeled in this system.
